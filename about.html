What rice millers look for in a plant partner

Setting up or upgrading a rice mill is a large investment. This is how we work to deliver on it.

ISO 9001:2015 certified quality

Our processes run under a certified quality management system, so planning, supply and commissioning follow documented, repeatable standards.

Parboiling, steaming and drying specialists

Parboiling and drying decide your head rice yield, colour and breakage. This is our core focus, not an add-on to general machinery supply.

One partner, start to finish

Layout, technology selection, integration, installation and trial runs handled together, so you are not coordinating many vendors.

Designed around your project

Capacity, paddy variety, product type (raw, steam or parboiled), space and budget shape the design.

Better recovery, less breakage

Correct soaking, steaming and drying are planned for higher whole-grain recovery, uniform colour and consistent quality.

Efficient use of fuel and power

Boiler, steam and dryer systems are matched to the plant to keep running costs under control, including husk-fired options where suitable.

Clear scope and timelines

A defined scope of supply, a practical schedule and regular progress updates, so you know what is included and when your mill will start.

Support after commissioning

Operator guidance, spare parts and service support from a team based in Bihar, close to the mills of eastern India.

A clear path from first discussion to a running plant

  1. Plant planning

    Capacity, layout and product goals defined with you.

  2. Technology selection

    Parboiling, steaming and drying systems matched to your paddy.

  3. Machinery integration

    Cleaning, boiler, dryer and milling sections working as one line.

  4. Installation

    Erection of plant and machinery at your site.

  5. Commissioning support

    Trial runs, tuning and operator guidance until output is stable.
