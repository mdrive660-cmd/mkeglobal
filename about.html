About Maa Kamakhya Enterprises

Practical solutions for rice processing.

Maa Kamakhya Enterprises provides rice mill setup and processing solutions with specialized expertise in paddy parboiling, steaming and drying technologies.

Our focus is on plant planning, technology selection, machinery integration, installation and commissioning support suited to each project's requirements.

An ISO 9001:2015 Certified Company

Why you should choose us

Setting up or upgrading a rice mill is a large investment. These are the things rice millers tell the industry they look for in a plant partner, and how we work to deliver them.

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality

    Our processes are run under a certified quality management system, so planning, supply and commissioning follow documented, repeatable standards.

  • Parboiling, steaming and drying specialists

    Parboiling and drying decide your head rice yield, colour and breakage. This is our core focus, not an add-on to general machinery supply.

  • One partner from planning to commissioning

    Plant layout, technology selection, machinery integration, installation and trial runs handled together, so you are not coordinating many vendors.

  • Plants designed around your project

    Capacity, paddy variety, product type (raw, steam or parboiled), available space and budget shape the design. We do not push a one-size-fits-all package.

  • Better recovery, less breakage

    Correct soaking, steaming and drying are planned for higher whole-grain recovery, uniform colour and consistent output quality.

  • Efficient use of fuel and power

    Boiler, steam and dryer systems are matched to the plant to keep running costs under control, including husk-fired options where suitable.

  • Clear scope and timelines

    A defined scope of supply, a practical schedule and regular progress updates, so you know what is included and when your mill will start.

  • Support after commissioning

    Operator guidance, spare parts and service support from a team based in Bihar, close to the mills of eastern India.

Our quality commitment. As an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, we work to a quality management system focused on consistent delivery, customer satisfaction and continual improvement.
