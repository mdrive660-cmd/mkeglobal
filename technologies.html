Our technologies

Processing technology and plant integration, built around the three stages that decide your rice quality.

Paddy parboiling

Parboiling, together with steaming and drying, decides head rice yield, colour and breakage. We plan the soaking and parboiling stages around your paddy and your product.

What we plan

  • Soaking and parboiling stages sized to your capacity
  • Systems matched to your paddy variety and product type
  • Planned for higher whole-grain recovery and uniform colour
Steaming photoimages/technologies/steaming.jpg

Steaming

Steaming equipment and boiler systems matched to the rest of the plant, so the process stays consistent and running costs stay under control.

What we plan

  • Boiler and steam systems matched to the plant
  • Efficient use of fuel and power
  • Husk-fired options where suitable
Drying photoimages/technologies/drying.jpg

Drying

Drying solutions that prepare parboiled paddy for the milling operations that follow, planned together with the rest of the line.

What we plan

  • Dryer systems matched to your plant capacity
  • Integrated with the cleaning and milling sections
  • Husk-fired options where suitable
